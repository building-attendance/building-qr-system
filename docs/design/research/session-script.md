# Script for a short session with a person who uses the apps

Five sessions of 10 to 15 minutes, with service providers and committee members, are the baseline for the questions
that no automated check can answer: can people finish their tasks, how long does it take, and where do they hesitate.
Write the findings into [`../baseline.md`](../baseline.md) as described at the end. The sessions are held in Hebrew (or
the person's language); this page is the English record of what is asked and recorded.

## Before the session

- Say: we are testing the app, not you; there are no wrong answers; please think aloud.
- Use test data only. A provider uses the demo account and a test point. A committee member creates a point or a
  provider named as a test, and the owner deletes it after the session; the visit to cancel is one of the demo account
  (the History tab hides the demo account until "Include demo account" is ticked).
- Record nothing that identifies the person: no name, no phone number, no picture of the screen with real data. Call
  them "provider 1", "committee 2" and so on.

## Tasks

A provider, on their own phone:

1. Sign in and record a visit at the point.
2. Record a visit in airplane mode, then turn airplane mode off.
3. Explain what the "too far from the point" message means (show a picture of it).

A committee member, on a computer or a phone:

1. Add a point with its location.
2. Find how many visits a given provider made last week.
3. Cancel a wrong visit.
4. Create a provider and hand them their password.

## What to record for each task

- Completed: alone, with help, or not.
- Time, from the end of the instruction to done.
- Where the person hesitated or made a mistake, in one line.
- How easy it was, from 1 (very hard) to 7 (very easy): the Single Ease Question.
- One quote, if there is a telling one, without anything that identifies the person.

## How the facilitator behaves

- Do not help until the person is completely stuck, and note that help was given.
- Do not explain the screen in advance.
- Do not defend the app: write down what the person says.

## Writing it into the baseline

In `../baseline.md`, under the date of the sessions, one line per task:
- how many of the people who attempted it completed it alone, out of how many (each task is done by one role only, for
  example "2 of 3 providers");
- the median time;
- the median ease;
- the most common hesitation.

No per-person rows.
